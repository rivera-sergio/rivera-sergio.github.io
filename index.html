Sergio A. Rivera

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics, University of Maryland

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics (University of Maryland) specializing in labor, firm dynamics, productivity, and financial frictions. I combine rigorous structural modeling with advanced computational tools — Stata, Python, and SAS — to analyze large-scale administrative microdata. Over ten years of experience producing high-stakes quantitative research for the World Bank and the Central Bank of Colombia, with expertise in building reproducible data pipelines for complex empirical analysis.

Focus areas

Research

Heterogeneous-firm general equilibrium models, labor market frictions, and misallocation in developing economies, using large administrative datasets to identify structural bottlenecks to firm growth.

Policy & Consulting

Quantitative consulting for the World Bank Group and the Inter-American Development Bank — gender gap decompositions, labor market impact assessments, and applied data pipelines for policy audiences.
