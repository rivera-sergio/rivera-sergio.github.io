Consultant
World Bank Group — Poverty and Equity Department
2021–2022, Summer 2023
  • Applied advanced econometric methods to dissect the income gender gap in Haiti and multiple West African countries, uncovering key links to economic activity, informality, and household characteristics.
World Bank Group — Gender Group
2019–2021
  • Led a team effort to build an algorithm systematizing the calculation of frequently used gender statistics — now the tool used by the team to produce the latest global gender-gap figures.
  • Created thousands of charts showcasing gender gaps across and within countries in labor, education, political participation, and other human development outcomes.
  • Provided evidence on the effects of forced displacement on attitudes and behaviors related to bargaining power and contraception.
